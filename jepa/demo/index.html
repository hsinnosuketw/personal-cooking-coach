Scrambled Eggs Coach V-JEPA 2 · Muse Glimmer · d1

A world model watches someone cook and predicts their next action; a coach LLM reads that and the recipe and says what to do next; a decision model turns the advice into one concrete next action, every second.

1 · World model

V-JEPA 2 + Meta's EPIC-KITCHENS probe. Every second it reads the last 3.2 s of video and predicts the action ~0.8 s ahead. 0.47 s per prediction (T4, fp16)
→

2 · Coach

Muse Glimmer 30B, reasoning low. Reads the recipe, what the cook has done and the prediction; says what to do next and why. Asked when the step tracker changes step or after 60 s.
→

3 · Decision model

Liquid AI d1-2B. Every second, reads the latest advice + the newest prediction and picks the next action from the recipe's Actions Bank, the next step, and whether to remind the cook now. 57 ms per decision (A100)
Advice appears only once the coach has actually answered, so the delay you see is real.

Choosing the coach setting

Each Glimmer setting was asked at the same moments (only the past was visible) to end with STEP: n. Correct = the step this cook worked on next, or the next step in recipe order. Latency on NVIDIA NIM, one call at a time. With so few moments at temperature 1, 1–2 answers apart is noise.

Full reasoning was no more accurate and ~6× slower, and it needs ~3,000 tokens, so smaller max_tokens caps cut its answers off. Reasoning low with max_tokens 1024 answers every time in ~500 tokens. Served on our own A100 (FP8) it takes per call instead of ~32 s on NIM. No setting picks the step better than the step tracker alone: the coach's value is the advice, not the step number.
