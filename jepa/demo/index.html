Scrambled Eggs Coach V-JEPA 2 · Muse Glimmer · d1

A world model watches someone cook and predicts their next action; a coach LLM reads that and the recipe and says what to do next; a decision model turns the advice into one concrete next action, every second.

1 · World Model

The world model inspects the actions the user has done and predicts the user's next move.

  • World model: V-JEPA 2 (ViT-L)
  • Probing classifier: V-JEPA 2 attentive probe for EPIC-KITCHENS-100 action anticipation

Every second it takes the last 3.2 s of video (32 frames), imagines the scene about 0.8 s ahead in its latent space, and the probe names the most likely actions (e.g. take egg); each prediction takes 0.47 s on a T4 GPU.

→

2 · Coach

The coach understands where the cook is in the recipe and tells the cook what to do next and why.

  • Coach model: Muse Glimmer (30B, reasoning low)

When the step changes, or after 60 s without advice, it reads the recipe, what the cook has done so far (the world model's recent predictions) and the current prediction, reasons briefly, and writes the next thing to do and why; an answer takes about 14 s.

→

3 · Decision Model

The decision model combines the coach's advice with the user's actions that the world model predicts, and turns them into one concrete next move, every second.

  • Decision model: Liquid AI d1-2B

Every second it reads the coach's latest advice and reasoning, the world model's prediction and the current step, then in one pass scores every action in the recipe's Actions Bank, picks the next step and decides whether to remind the cook now; a decision takes 57 ms on an A100 GPU.
