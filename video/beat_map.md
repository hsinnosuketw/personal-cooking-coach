# Beat map (draft 2)

One home for the beat map: video/review/build_review.py renders it on the review page and writes video/beat_map.md from it. Times are estimates until the TTS files exist. 'say' is the narrator unless a line starts with 'Coach:' (the coach's own, flatter voice). 'screen' describes everything visible. Words that appear on screen are in double quotes and stay at 5 or fewer per screen; eyebrows such as NO MEMORY · 13:28 are evidence labels and don't count.

## 0:00 · The background

**What we say**: Anyone learning to cook knows the juggle: chop one thing, check the recipe, start another, then come back.

**What's on screen**: Three quick full-screen shots from the learner's head camera, one per phrase: opening the fridge, tapping a phone beside the kitchen scale, slicing a red onion. No text at all.

**Clips**: M1, N2, M2 · **Sound**: Warm music bed starts low.

## 0:08 · The problem

**What we say**: A good cooking teacher keeps track of all that for you. An AI coach without memory can't. This one told our learner to crack the eggs fourteen times. The eggs were already in the pan.

**What's on screen**: Eggs cooking in the pan, full screen. A small white coach card slides in top-left: eyebrow NO MEMORY · 13:28, line "Crack the eggs." A big red counter beside it rolls from ×1 to "×14" while the line is spoken.

**Clips**: N6 · **Sound**: A soft tick on each count.

## 0:20 · The promise

**What we say**: Ours remembers what you've done, so you can learn your way.

**What's on screen**: The frame splits into two squares on a dark background. Left: the eggs in the pan with the red card. Right: the learner chopping onion, with a green card, eyebrow MEMORY · 1:45, line "Keep going." Under them: "No memory" and "Memory".

**Clips**: N6, M2 · **Sound**: Pop as the green card appears.

## 0:26 · Chapter 1 · It repeats

**What we say**: Without memory, a coach repeats itself. Coach: Crack the eggs. Crack the eggs. Crack the eggs.

**What's on screen**: On a pale green paper card, a red numbered square "1" and the title "Without memory" for one second. Then three hard cuts: the onion on the chopping board, the phone, the oil spray. Each shot gets the same red card "Crack the eggs." and the counter steps "×1", "×2", "×3".

**Clips**: N1, N2, N3 · **Sound**: Pop on each card.

**What's on screen**: Close-up of the learner cracking eggs into a bowl. At the bottom, the egg step icon fills green with "5:13". Hard cut to 8:54: the bowl of whisked eggs sits by the stove while the learner fetches a plate; the red card returns, eyebrow NO MEMORY · 8:54, "Crack the eggs."

**Clips**: N4, N5 · **Sound**: Tick as the icon fills, pop for the card.

## 0:48 · It can't tell what's done

**What's on screen**: The learner scrapes the scrambled eggs onto a plate. The red card appears: eyebrow NO MEMORY · 13:59, "Add the onions." The shot holds on the finished plate for a moment.

## 1:04 · Chapter 2 · Learn your way

**What we say**: Start wherever you like. Our learner gets the plate ready first, then chops the onion, and the coach goes along: fine, do it in parallel.

**What's on screen**: A green numbered square "2" and the title "With memory" for one second. The learner takes ingredients from the fridge; green card, eyebrow MEMORY · 0:39, "Plate first? Fine." Hard cut to the onion being chopped; card "Keep going."

**Clips**: M1, M2 · **Sound**: The music returns; pop on each card.

## 1:17 · It remembers

**What we say**: Every action it sees is written down against a recipe step, so it always knows what has started and what is left.

**What's on screen**: A row of seven round step icons appears along the bottom, all grey outlines. Four two-second shots follow (the plate, the onion, the pan, the eggs), and with each one its icon fills green and shows the moment it was first seen: 0:13, 1:50, 4:36, 5:13.

**Clips**: graphic only · **Sound**: A light tick on each fill.

## 1:28 · Only when it's needed

**What we say**: It only speaks up when something is needed. The onions will need a hot pan, so it reminds you to oil the skillet. A couple of minutes later, you do.

**What's on screen**: The onion chopping continues; a green card, eyebrow MEMORY · 2:07, "Oil the pan first." Hard cut to the learner spraying oil into the pan; the pan icon in the step row fills with "4:36".

**Clips**: M4, M5 · **Sound**: Pop, then tick.

**What we say**: Here's how. Meta's V-JEPA 2 world model watches the video and answers: what is the learner about to do? Muse Glimmer, the coach, reads the memory and answers: what matters now? And Liquid AI's d1 turns that into one next move, every second.

**What we say**: The secret is the memory. Each action, take egg, cut onion, spray oil, is mapped back to its recipe step, with the moment it was first seen.

**What's on screen**: On paper, a timeline of small action chips runs across the top ("take egg", "cut onion", "spray oil"). As each is spoken, its chip lifts off and drops into the matching slot of the step row below, which then shows its first-seen time.

**Clips**: graphic only · **Sound**: Tick per landing.
